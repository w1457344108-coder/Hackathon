# Phase 1 Demo Video Design

## Style Prompt

Create a restrained legal-research demo video for non-CS legal reviewers. The video should feel clear, evidence-first, and presentation-ready: white paper surfaces, dark ink typography, muted navy accents, thin evidence-link lines, and calm card-based motion. Avoid technical clutter; explain the system as documents becoming reviewable evidence cards and traceable answers.

## Colors

- Paper background: `#F7F8FA`
- Ink text: `#111418`
- Muted text: `#596273`
- Navy accent: `#123A63`
- Evidence blue: `#2D6CDF`
- Soft border: `#D8DEE8`
- Risk red: `#B42318`
- Review green: `#16885D`

## Typography

- Primary: Arial / Helvetica Neue / system sans-serif
- Captions: Arial Bold, high contrast, white on dark translucent panel

## What Not To Do

- Do not show Chinese text in generated cards or captions.
- Do not use code-heavy screenshots or unexplained CS terminology.
- Do not use decorative gradients, orbs, or busy backgrounds.
- Do not imply that the prototype is final legal advice.
- Do not add music or voiceover in this version.
