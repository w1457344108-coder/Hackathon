Company to keep copies of charging instruments and register of charges
138.—(1) Every company must cause the instrument creating any charge requiring
registration under this Division or a copy thereof to be kept at the registered office of the
company for as long as the charge to which the instrument relates remains in force, but in
the case of a series of debentures the keeping of a copy of one debenture of the series is
sufficient for the purposes of this subsection.
[36/2014]
(1A) An instrument creating any charge or a copy thereof, or a copy of the series of
debentures, as the case may be, that is required to be kept under subsection (1) —
(a)
is deemed to form part of the records that are required to be kept under
section 199(1); and
(b)
for the purposes of section 199(2), must be retained by the company for a
