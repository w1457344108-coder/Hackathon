Rectification by Court
12B.—(1) Where it appears to the Court, as a result of evidence adduced before it by
an applicant company, that any particular recorded in a register is erroneous or defective,
the Court may, by order, direct the Registrar to rectify the register on such terms and
conditions as seem to the Court just and expedient, as are specified in the order and the
Registrar must, upon receipt of the order, rectify the register accordingly.
[36/2014; 40/2019]
(2) An order of the Court made under subsection (1) may require that a fresh
document, showing the rectification, must be filed by the applicant company with the
Registrar together with a copy of the Court order, and a copy of the Court application.
