5. The collection, use or disclosure (as the case may be) of personal data about an individual
is necessary for the provision of legal services by the organisation to another person, or for the
organisation to obtain legal services.
6.—(1) Subject to sub-paragraph (2), the collection, use or disclosure (as the case may be) of
personal data about an individual —
(a)
is for the purpose of the preparation by a credit bureau of a credit report; or
(b)
relates to a credit report provided by a credit bureau to a member of the credit bureau
in relation to a transaction between the member and the individual.
(2) Sub-paragraph (1) does not apply to a credit bureau that, being required to obtain a
licence under any other written law, does not hold such a licence.
