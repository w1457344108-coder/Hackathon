Section 50(2)
POWERS OF INVESTIGATION OF COMMISSION AND INSPECTORS
Power to require documents or information
1.—(1) For the purposes of an investigation under section 50, the Commission or an inspector may,
by written notice to any organisation, require the organisation to produce to the Commission or the
inspector a specified document or specified information, which the Commission or inspector considers
relates to any matter relevant to such investigation.
(2) A notice under sub-paragraph (1) must indicate the purpose for which the specified document or
specified information is required by the Commission.
(3) The Commission may specify in the notice —
(a)
the time and place at which any document is to be produced or any information is to be
provided; and
(b)
the manner and form in which it is to be produced or provided.
