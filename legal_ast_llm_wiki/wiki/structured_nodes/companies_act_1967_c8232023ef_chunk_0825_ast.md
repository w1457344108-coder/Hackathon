(1A) Subject to subsections (1B) and (1C), the Minister must give a copy of a report
made under this Part to each person to whom in the opinion of the Minister the report
ought to be given by reason that it relates to the affairs of that person to a material extent.
(1B) The Minister is not bound to furnish a company, an applicant or any other
person with a copy of the report or any part thereof if the Minister is of the opinion that
there is good reason for not divulging the contents of the report or any part thereof.
(1C) Subject to subsection (1D), the Minister must not give a copy of a report made
under this Part to a person under subsection (1A) if the Minister believes that legal
proceedings that have been or, in the Minister’s opinion, might be instituted, might be
unduly prejudiced by giving the report to that person.
