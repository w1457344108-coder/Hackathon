Company’s duty to notify members that resolution passed by written means
184E.—(1) Where a resolution of a private company or an unlisted public company is
passed by written means, the company must —
(a)
notify every member that it has been passed; and
(b)
do so within 15 days from the earliest date on which a director or secretary
of the company is aware that it has been passed.
[36/2014]
(2) Non-compliance with this section does not render the resolution invalid.
