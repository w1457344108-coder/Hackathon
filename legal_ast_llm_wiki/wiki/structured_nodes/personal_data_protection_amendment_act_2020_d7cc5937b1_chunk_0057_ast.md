Unauthorised disclosure of personal data
48D.—(1) If —
(a)
an individual discloses, or the individual’s conduct causes disclosure
of, personal data in the possession or under the control of an
organisation or a public agency to another person;
(b)
the disclosure is not authorised by the organisation or public agency,
as the case may be; and
(c)
the individual does so —
(i)
knowing that the disclosure is not authorised by the
organisation or public agency, as the case may be; or
(ii)
reckless as to whether the disclosure is or is not authorised
by the organisation or public agency, as the case may be,
the individual shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a
fine not exceeding $5,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or
