(7) The conversion of a company referred to in subsection (6) is effected by lodging
with the Registrar a special resolution determining the conversion of the company from a
company limited by guarantee with a share capital to a company limited by shares or to a
company limited by guarantee (as the case may be) and altering its constitution to the
extent that is necessary to bring them into conformity with the requirements of this Act
relating to the constitution of a company limited by shares or of a company limited by
guarantee, as the case may be.
