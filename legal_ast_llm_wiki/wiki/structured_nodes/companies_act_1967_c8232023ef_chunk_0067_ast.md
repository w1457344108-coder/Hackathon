Invitation to lend money deemed invitation to purchase debentures
(5) For the purposes of this Act, any invitation to the public to deposit money with or
lend money to a corporation (other than a corporation that is a prescribed entity
mentioned in section 239(4) of the Securities and Futures Act 2001 is deemed to be an
invitation to subscribe for or purchase debentures of the corporation.
(5A) For the purposes of this Act, any document that is issued or intended or required
to be issued by a corporation acknowledging or evidencing or constituting an
acknowledgment of the indebtedness of the corporation in respect of any money that is or
may be deposited with or lent to the corporation in response to such an invitation is
deemed to be a debenture.
