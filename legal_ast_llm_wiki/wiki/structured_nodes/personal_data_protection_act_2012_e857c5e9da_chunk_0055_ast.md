## Original Excerpt

```text
imiting subsection (1)(a), a data breach is deemed to result in significant
harm to an individual —
(a)
if the data breach is in relation to any prescribed personal data or class of
personal data relating to the individual; or
(b)
in other prescribed circumstances.
[40/2020]
(3) Without limiting subsection (1)(b), a data breach is deemed to be of a significant
scale —
(a)
if the data breach affects not fewer than the prescribed number of affected
```
