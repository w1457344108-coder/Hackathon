Branch registers
196.—(1) A public company having a share capital may cause to be kept in any place
outside Singapore a branch register of members which is deemed to be part of the
company’s register of members.
[36/2014]
(2) The public company must lodge with the Registrar notice of the situation of the
office where any branch register is kept and of any change in its situation, and if it is
