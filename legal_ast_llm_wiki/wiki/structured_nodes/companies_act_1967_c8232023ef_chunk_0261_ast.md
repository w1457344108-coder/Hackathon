```text
Conversion of shares
74A.—(1) Subject to this section and sections 64A and 75, a company the share
capital of which is divided into different classes of shares may make provision in its
constitution to authorise the conversion of one class of shares into another class of
shares.
[36/2014]
(2) A public company may convert one class of shares (A) into another class of shares
(B) by special resolution only if the constitution of the public company —
(a)
permits B to be issued; and
(b)
sets out the rights attached to B.
[36/2014]
(3) A private company may convert shares from one class to another by lodging a
notice of conversion in the prescribed form with the Registrar.
[36/2014]
(4) A conversion of shares by a private company on or after 3 January 2016 does not
take effect until the electronic register of members of the company is updated by the
Registrar under section 196A(5).
[36/2014]
(5) Section 74 applies where a conversion of shares undertaken by a company
```
