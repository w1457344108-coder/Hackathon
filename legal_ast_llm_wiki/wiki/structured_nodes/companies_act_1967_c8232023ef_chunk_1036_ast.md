Restriction on the use of word “Private” or “Sendirian”
(2) A company must not use the word “Private” or “Sendirian” or any abbreviation
thereof as part of its name if it does not fulfil the requirements required by this Act to be
fulfilled by private companies and every corporation and every officer of a corporation
who is in default shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine
not exceeding $5,000 and also to a default penalty.
Penalty for holding out business as registered foreign company
(3) If a person carrying on a business, the person’s agent or a person acting on the
firstmentioned person’s behalf, in any way holds out that the business is registered as a
foreign company under this Act when at the material time the business was not so
registered, that person shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a
fine not exceeding $10,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 2 years or to
both.
