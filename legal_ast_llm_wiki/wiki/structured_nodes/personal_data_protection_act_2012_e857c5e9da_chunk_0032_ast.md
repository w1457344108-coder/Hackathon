```text
(2) An organisation must not —
(a)
as a condition of providing a product or service, require an individual to
consent to the collection, use or disclosure of personal data about the
individual beyond what is reasonable to provide the product or service to
that individual; or
(b)
obtain or attempt to obtain consent for collecting, using or disclosing
personal data by providing false or misleading information with respect to
the collection, use or disclosure of the personal data, or using deceptive or
misleading practices.
(3) Any consent given in any of the circumstances in subsection (2) is not validly
given for the purposes of this Act.
(4) In this Act, references to consent given, or deemed to have been given, by an
individual for the collection, use or disclosure of personal data about the individual
include consent given, or deemed to have been given, by any person validly acting on
that individual’s behalf for the collection, use or disclosure of such personal data.
```
