(5) For the purposes of any investigation under this section, the provisions of this Part
with respect to the investigation of declared companies apply with the necessary
modifications of references to the affairs of the corporation or to those of any other
corporation, but so that —
(a)
this Part applies in relation to all persons who are or have been, or whom
the inspector has reasonable cause to believe to be or to have been
financially interested in the success or failure or the apparent success or
failure of the corporation or any other corporation the membership of
which is investigated with that of the corporation, or able to control or
materially to influence the policy thereof, including persons concerned
only on behalf of others, as they apply in relation to officers and agents of
the corporation or of the other corporation, as the case may be; and
(b)
the Minister is not bound to furnish the corporation or any other person
