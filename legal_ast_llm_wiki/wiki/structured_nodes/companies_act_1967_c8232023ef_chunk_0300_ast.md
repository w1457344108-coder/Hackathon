76B.—(1) Despite section 76, a company may, in accordance with this section and
sections 76C to 76G, purchase or otherwise acquire shares issued by it if it is expressly
permitted to do so by its constitution.
[36/2014]
(2) This section and sections 76C to 76G apply to ordinary shares, stocks and
preference shares.
(3) The total number of ordinary shares and stocks in any class that may be purchased
or acquired by a company during the relevant period must not exceed 20% (or such other

shares and stocks of the company in that class ascertained as at the date of any resolution
