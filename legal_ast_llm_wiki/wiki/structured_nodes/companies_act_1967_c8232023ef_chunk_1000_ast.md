386A. In this section and sections 387B, 387C, 397 and 401, unless the contrary
intention appears —
“consolidated financial statements” and “parent company” have the meanings
given by section 209A;
“financial statements” means the financial statements of a company required to be
prepared by the Accounting Standards and, in the case of a parent company,
means the consolidated financial statements.
