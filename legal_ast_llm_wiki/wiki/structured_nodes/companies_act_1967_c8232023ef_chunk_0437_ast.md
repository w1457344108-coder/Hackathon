```text
(1A) The conditions to be complied with by a person referred to in subsection (1) are
the following:
(a)
the person has, by himself or herself or through a registered qualified
individual authorised by him or her, filed with the Registrar —
(i)
a declaration that he or she has consented to act as a director;
(ii)
a statement in the prescribed form that he or she is not
disqualified from acting as a director under this Act; and
(iii)
a statement in the prescribed form that he or she is not debarred
under section 155B from acting as director of the company;
```
