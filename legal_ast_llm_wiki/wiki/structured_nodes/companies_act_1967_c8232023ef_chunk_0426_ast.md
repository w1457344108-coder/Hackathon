```text
PART 5
MANAGEMENT AND ADMINISTRATION
Division 1 — Office and name
Registered office of company
142.—(1) A company must as from the date of its incorporation have a registered
office within Singapore to which all communications and notices may be addressed and
which must be open and accessible to the public for not less than 3 hours during ordinary
business hours on each business day.
(2) If default is made in complying with subsection (1), the company and every
officer of the company who is in default shall be guilty of an offence and shall be liable
on conviction to a fine not exceeding $5,000 and also to a default penalty.
```
