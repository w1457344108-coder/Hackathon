## Original Excerpt

```text
the appointment of
2 or more persons as directors by a single resolution must not be made unless a
resolution that it may be so made has first been agreed to by the meeting without any
vote being given against it.
(2) A resolution passed pursuant to a motion made in contravention of this section is
void, whether or not its being so moved was objected to at the time.
(3) Where a resolution pursuant to a motion made in contravention of this section is
passed, no provision for the automatic re-appointment of retiring directors in default of
another appointment is to apply.
(4) For the purposes of this section, a motion for approving a person’s appointment or
for nominating a person for appointment is to be treated as a motion for the person’s
appointment.
(5) Nothing in this section —
(a)
applies to a resolution altering the company’s constitution;
(b)
prevents the election of 2 or more directors by ballot or poll.
[36/2014]
```
