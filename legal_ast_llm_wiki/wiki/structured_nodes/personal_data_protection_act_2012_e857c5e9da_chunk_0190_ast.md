the member of the Appeal Panel appointed by the Chairperson of the Appeal Panel as the
presiding member of that Appeal Committee.
(2) However, in the absence at any meeting of the presiding member of an Appeal Committee
mentioned in sub-paragraph (1), another member of the Appeal Committee chosen by the members of
that Appeal Committee present is to preside at that meeting.
(3) All matters coming before an Appeal Committee are to be decided by a majority of votes of those
members present and, in the event of an equality of votes, the presiding member has a second or casting
vote.
