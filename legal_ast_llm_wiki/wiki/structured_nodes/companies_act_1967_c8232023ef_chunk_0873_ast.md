“notice of transfer of registration” means the notice of transfer of registration
issued under section 359(3);
“place of incorporation” means, in the case of a foreign corporate entity that had
transferred its domicile after its incorporation, the jurisdiction where the
foreign corporate entity is domiciled at the time it applies for registration;
“registration”, in relation to a foreign corporate entity that has applied to be
registered as a company limited by shares under this Part, means registration
by the Registrar under section 359(1), and “register” and “registered” are to be
construed accordingly.
[15/2017]
