50.—(1) The Commission may, upon complaint or of its own motion, conduct an
investigation under this section to determine whether or not an organisation or a person is
complying with this Act, including a voluntary undertaking given by the organisation or
person under section 48L(1).

inspectors are set out in the Ninth Schedule.
