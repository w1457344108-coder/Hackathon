```text
157.—(1) A director must at all times act honestly and use reasonable diligence in the
discharge of the duties of his or her office.
(2) An officer or agent of a company must not make improper use of his or her
position as an officer or agent of the company or any information acquired by virtue of
his or her position as an officer or agent of the company to gain, directly or indirectly, an
advantage for himself or herself or for any other person or to cause detriment to the
company.
[36/2014]
(3) An officer or agent who commits a breach of any of the provisions of this section
shall be —
(a)
liable to the company for any profit made by him or her or for any damage
suffered by the company as a result of the breach of any of those
provisions; and
(b)
guilty of an offence and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not
exceeding $20,000 or to imprisonment for a term not exceeding 12 months
or to both.
[Act 24 of 2025 wef 06/05/2026]
(4) This section is in addition to and not in derogation of any other written law or rule
```
