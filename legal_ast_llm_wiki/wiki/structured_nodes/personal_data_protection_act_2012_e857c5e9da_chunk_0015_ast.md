## Original Excerpt

```text
(c)
a circumstance or conduct that may result in a remedy or relief being
available under any law;
“national interest” includes national defence, national security, public security, the
maintenance of essential services and the conduct of international affairs;
“organisation” includes any individual, company, association or body of persons,
corporate or unincorporated, whether or not —
(a)
formed or recognised under the law of Singapore; or
(b)
resident, or having an office or a place of business, in Singapore;
“personal data” means data, whether true or not, about an individual who can be
identified —
(a)
from that data; or
(b)
from that data and other information to which the organisation has or
is likely to have access;
```
