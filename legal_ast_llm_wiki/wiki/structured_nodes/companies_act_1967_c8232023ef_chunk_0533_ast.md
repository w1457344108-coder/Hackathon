(8) Nothing in section 164 or this section requires a company to enter in its register or

(9) Any director or chief executive officer who fails to comply with subsection (1) or
(2) or any company that fails to comply with subsection (3) shall be guilty of an offence
and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $15,000 or to imprisonment for
a term not exceeding 3 years and, in the case of a continuing offence, to a further fine of
$1,000 for every day during which the offence continues after conviction.
