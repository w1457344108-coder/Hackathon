Size, durability and legibility of documents delivered to Registrar
15.—(1) For the purposes of securing that the documents delivered to the Registrar
under the provisions of this Act are of a standard size, durable and easily legible, the
Minister may by regulations prescribe such requirements (whether as to size, weight,
quality or colour of paper, size, type or colour of lettering, or otherwise) as the Minister
may consider appropriate; and different requirements may be so prescribed for different
documents or classes of documents.
