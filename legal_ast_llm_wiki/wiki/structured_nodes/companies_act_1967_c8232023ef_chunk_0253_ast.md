Effect of redenomination
73A.—(1) A redenomination of shares does not affect —
(a)
any rights or obligations of members under the company’s constitution or
any restrictions affecting members under the company’s constitution; or
(b)
any entitlement to dividends (including any entitlement to dividends in a
particular currency), voting rights and liability in respect of amounts
remaining unpaid on shares (including liability in a particular currency).
