VENTH SCHEDULE
Section 48P(5)
CONSTITUTION AND PROCEEDINGS OF DATA PROTECTION APPEAL PANEL AND DATA
PROTECTION APPEAL COMMITTEES
Data Protection Appeal Panel
1.—(1) The Data Protection Appeal Panel consists of not more than 30 members appointed by the
Minister on the basis of their ability and experience in industry, commerce or administration or their
professional qualifications or their suitability otherwise for appointment.
(2) Members of the Appeal Panel are appointed for such period as the Minister may determine and
are eligible for re-appointment.
