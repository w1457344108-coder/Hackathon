(5) For the purposes of subsection (4A), a person is an associate of another person if
the firstmentioned person is —
(a)
a subsidiary of that other person;
(b)
a person who is accustomed or is under an obligation whether formal or
informal to act in accordance with the directions, instructions or wishes of
that other person in relation to the share mentioned in subsection (4A); or
(c)
a body corporate that is, or a majority of the directors of which are,
accustomed or under an obligation whether formal or informal to act in
