11. Paragraph 10 does not apply —
(a)
to a group that has not reached its third financial year after it is formed; or
(b)
in the case of a group that was formed before 1 July 2015, to a group that has not reached its
third financial year after that date.
12. For the purposes of this Schedule —
(a)
the question whether an entity is part of a group is to be decided in accordance with the
Accounting Standards;

in the case —
(i)
where consolidated financial statements are prepared by a parent in relation to a
