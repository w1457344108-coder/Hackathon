Division 2 — Offences
400. [Repealed by S 236/2002]
False and misleading statement
401.—(1) Every corporation which advertises, circulates or publishes any statement
of the amount of its capital which is misleading, or in which the amount of capital or
subscribed capital is stated but the amount of paid-up capital or the amount of any charge
on uncalled capital is not stated as prominently as the amount of subscribed capital is
stated, and every officer of the corporation who knowingly authorises, directs or consents
to such advertising, circulation or publication shall be guilty of an offence.
(2) Every person who in any return, report, certificate, balance sheet, financial
statements or other document required by or for the purposes of this Act wilfully makes
or authorises the making of a statement false or misleading in any material particular
knowing it to be false or misleading or wilfully omits or authorises the omission of any
matter or thing without which the document is misleading in a material respect shall be
