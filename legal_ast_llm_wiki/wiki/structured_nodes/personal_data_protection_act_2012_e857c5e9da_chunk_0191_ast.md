Powers of Appeal Committees
4.—(1) An Appeal Committee has all the powers and duties of the Commission that are necessary to
perform its functions and discharge its duties under this Act.
(2) An Appeal Committee has the powers, rights and privileges vested in a District Court on the
hearing of an action, including —
(a)
the enforcement of the attendance of witnesses and their examination on oath or otherwise;
(b)
the compelling of the production of documents; and
(c)
