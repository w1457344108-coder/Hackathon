Power to entrench provisions of constitution of company
26A.—(1) An entrenching provision may —
(a)
be included in the constitution with which a company is formed; and
(b)
at any time be inserted in the constitution of a company only if all the
members of the company agree.
[36/2014]
(2) An entrenching provision may be removed or altered only if all the members of
the company agree.
[36/2014]
(3) The provisions of this Act relating to the alteration of the constitution of a
company are subject to any entrenching provision in the constitution of a company.
[36/2014]
(4) In this section, “entrenching provision” means a provision of the constitution of a
company to the effect that other specified provisions of the constitution —
(a)
may not be altered in the manner provided by this Act; or
(b)
may not be so altered except —
(i)
by a resolution passed by a specified majority greater than 75%
(the minimum majority required by this Act for a special
resolution); or
(ii)
where other specified conditions are met.
[36/2014]
