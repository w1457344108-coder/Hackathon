26.—(1) An organisation must not transfer any personal data to a country or territory
outside Singapore except in accordance with requirements prescribed under this Act to
ensure that organisations provide a standard of protection to personal data so transferred
that is comparable to the protection under this Act.
(2) The Commission may, on the application of any organisation, by written notice

respect of any transfer of personal data by that organisation.

in writing; and
