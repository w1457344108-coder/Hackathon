, and in the case of a public
company having a share capital a statement of the shares held by each

member;
(c)
the date at which any person who ceased to be a member during the
previous 7 years so ceased to be a member; and
(d)
in the case of a public company having a share capital, the date of every
allotment of shares to members and the number of shares comprised in
each allotment.
