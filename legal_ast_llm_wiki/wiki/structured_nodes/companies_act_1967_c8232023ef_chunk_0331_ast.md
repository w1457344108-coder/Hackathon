Treasury shares: maximum holdings
76I.—(1) Where a company has shares of only one class, the aggregate number of
shares held as treasury shares must not at any time exceed 10% of the total number of
shares of the company at that time.
(2) Where the share capital of a company is divided into shares of different classes,
the aggregate number of the shares of any class held as treasury shares must not at any
time exceed 10% of the total number of the shares in that class at that time.
(3) Where subsection (1) or (2) is contravened by a company, the company must
dispose of or cancel the excess shares in accordance with section 76K before the end of
the period of 6 months beginning with the day on which that contravention occurs, or
such further period as the Registrar may allow.
(4) In subsection (3), “the excess shares” means such number of the shares, held by
the company as treasury shares at the time in question, as resulted in the limit being
exceeded.
Treasury shares: voting and other rights
76J.—(1) This section applies to shares which are held by a company as treasury
shares.
