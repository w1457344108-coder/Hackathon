4. The collection, use or disclosure (as the case may be) of personal data about an individual is solely

sensitive to the individual to be collected, used or disclosed (as the case may be) at the proposed time.
5. The personal data about an individual is collected, used or disclosed (as the case may be) by a
news organisation solely for its news activity.
6. In this Part —
“broadcasting service” has the meaning given by section 2(1) of the Broadcasting Act 1994;
“news activity” means —
(a)
the gathering of news, or the preparation or compilation of articles or programmes
of or concerning news, observations on news, or current affairs, for the purposes
of dissemination to the public or any section of the public; or
(b)
the dissemination, to the public or any section of the public, of any article or
programme of or concerning —
(i)
news;
(ii)
observations on news; or
(iii)
current affairs;

(a)
