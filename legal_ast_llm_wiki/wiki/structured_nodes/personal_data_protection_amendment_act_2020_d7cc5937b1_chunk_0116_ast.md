## Original Excerpt

```text
must —
(a)
conduct an assessment, before collecting, using or disclosing the personal data (as the
case may be), to determine whether sub-paragraph (1) is satisfied; and
(b)
provide the individual with reasonable access to information about the organisation’s
collection, use or disclosure of personal data (as the case may be) in accordance with
sub-paragraph (1).
(3) The organisation must, in respect of the assessment mentioned in sub-paragraph (2)(a) —
(a)
identify any adverse effect that the proposed collection, use or disclosure (as the case
may be) of personal data about an individual is likely to have on the individual;
(b)
identify and implement reasonable measures —
(i)
to eliminate the adverse effect;
```
