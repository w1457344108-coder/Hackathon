Controller’s duty to provide information
386AJ.—(1) A person who knows or ought reasonably to know that the person is a
registrable controller in relation to a company or foreign company must —
(a)
notify the company or foreign company (as the case may be) that the
person is a registrable controller in relation to the company or foreign
company;
(b)
state the date, to the best of the person’s knowledge, on which the person
became a registrable controller in relation to the company or foreign
company; and
(c)
provide such other information as may be prescribed.
[15/2017]
