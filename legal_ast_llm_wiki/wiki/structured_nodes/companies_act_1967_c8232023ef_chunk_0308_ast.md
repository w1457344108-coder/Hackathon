Authority for off-market acquisition on equal access scheme
76C.—(1) A company, whether or not it is listed on an approved exchange in
Singapore or any securities exchange outside Singapore, may make a purchase or
acquisition of its own shares otherwise than on an approved exchange in Singapore or
any securities exchange outside Singapore (called in this section an off-market purchase)
