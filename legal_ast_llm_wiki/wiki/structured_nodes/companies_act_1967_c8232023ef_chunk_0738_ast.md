```text
(2) An auditor must, in a report under this section, state —
(a)
whether the financial statements and, if the company is a parent company
for which consolidated financial statements are prepared, the consolidated
financial statements are in the auditor’s opinion —
(i)
in compliance with the requirements of the Accounting
Standards; and
(ii)
give a true and fair view of —
(A)
the financial position and performance of the company;
and
(B)
if consolidated financial statements are required, the
financial position and performance of the group;
(aa)
if the financial statements or consolidated financial statements do not
comply with any requirement of the Accounting Standards and the
non-compliance is neither —
(i)
approved by the Registrar under section 201(12); nor
(ii)
allowed under an order by the Minister under section 201(15A),
whether the non-compliance is, in the opinion of the auditor, necessary for
the financial statements or consolidated financial statements to give a true
and fair view of any matter required by section 201 to be dealt with in
them;
```
