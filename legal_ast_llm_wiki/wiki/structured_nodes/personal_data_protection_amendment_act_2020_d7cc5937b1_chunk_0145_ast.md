“Interpretation of sections 14A, 14B and 14C
14. In sections 14A, 14B and 14C —
“gain” means —
(a)
a gain in property or a supply of services, whether temporary
or permanent; or
(b)
an opportunity to earn remuneration or greater remuneration or
to gain a financial advantage otherwise than by way of
remuneration;
“generally available information” means information that consists of readily
observable matter, including information that consists of deductions,
conclusions or inferences made or drawn from readily observable matter;
“harm”, in relation to an individual, means —
(a)
any physical harm; or
(b)
harassment, alarm or distress caused to the individual;
“loss” means —
(a)
a loss in property or a supply of services, whether temporary or
permanent; or
(b)
a loss of an opportunity to earn remuneration or greater
remuneration or to gain a financial advantage otherwise than
by way of remuneration,
but excludes, in relation to an individual, the loss of personal data about
the individual;
“personal data” has the meaning given by section 2(1) of the Personal Data
