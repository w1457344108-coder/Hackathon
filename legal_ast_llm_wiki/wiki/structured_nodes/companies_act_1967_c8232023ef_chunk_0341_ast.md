78B.—(1) A private company limited by shares may reduce its share capital in any
way by a special resolution if the company —
(a)
[Deleted by Act 36 of 2014]

section 78E.
[36/2014]
(2) Despite subsection (1), the company need not meet the solvency requirements if
the reduction of share capital does not involve any of the following:
(a)
a reduction or distribution of cash or other assets by the company;
(b)
a release of any liability owed to the company.
