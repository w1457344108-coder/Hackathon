2.—(1) The Chairperson of the Appeal Panel, unless his or her appointment is revoked by the

Minister may determine and is eligible for re-appointment.

the temporary incapacity from illness or otherwise or during the temporary absence from Singapore of
the Chairperson of the Appeal Panel.
Secretary to Appeal Panel
2A.—(1) The Secretary to the Appeal Panel is to be appointed by the Minister.
(2) The Secretary is to provide administrative and secretarial support to the Chairperson of the
Appeal Panel, the Appeal Panel and every Appeal Committee, in the discharge of their functions, duties
and powers under this Act.
