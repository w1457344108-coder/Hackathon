General penalties
56. A person guilty of an offence under this Act for which no penalty is expressly
provided shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $10,000 or to
imprisonment for a term not exceeding 3 years or to both and, in the case of a continuing
offence, to a further fine not exceeding $1,000 for every day or part of a day during
which the offence continues after conviction.
Public servants and public officers
57.—(1) All individuals appointed under section 8(1) —
(a)
