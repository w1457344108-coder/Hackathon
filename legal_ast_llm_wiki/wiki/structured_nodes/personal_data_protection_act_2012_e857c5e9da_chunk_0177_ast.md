(b)

SECOND SCHEDULE
Sections 2(1) and 17(1)
ADDITIONAL BASES FOR COLLECTION, USE AND
DISCLOSURE OF PERSONAL DATA WITHOUT CONSENT
PART 1
COLLECTION OF PERSONAL DATA
1. The collection of personal data about an individual, if —
(a)
the personal data was disclosed by a public agency; and
(b)
the collection of the personal data by the organisation is consistent with the purpose of the
disclosure by the public agency.

USE OF PERSONAL DATA
