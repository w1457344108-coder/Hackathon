investigated pursuant to this Part, fails to comply with the requirements of any notice
issued under subsection (3) or fails or refuses to answer any question which is put to him
or her by an inspector with respect to the affairs of the corporation or that officer or agent
is a director or past director to whom subsection (4A) applies, if he or she fails to comply
with a requirement of an inspector under that subsection, the inspector may certify the
failure or refusal under the hand of the inspector to the Court, which may thereupon
inquire into the case and, after hearing any witnesses against or on behalf of the alleged
offender and any statement offered in defence, punish the offender in like manner as if
the offender had been guilty of contempt of the Court.
