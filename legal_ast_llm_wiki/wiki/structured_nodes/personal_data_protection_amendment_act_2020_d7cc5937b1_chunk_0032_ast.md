Purpose of this Part
26G. The purpose of this Part is to —
(a)
provide individuals with greater autonomy and control over their
personal data; and
(b)
facilitate the innovative and more intensive use of applicable data in
the possession or under the control of organisations to support the
development, enhancement and refinement of goods and services
provided by other organisations located or operating in Singapore or
elsewhere.
