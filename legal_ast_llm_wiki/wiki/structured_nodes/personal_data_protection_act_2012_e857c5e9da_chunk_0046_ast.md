21.—(1) Subject to subsections (2), (3) and (4), on request of an individual, an
organisation must, as soon as reasonably possible, provide the individual with —
(a)
personal data about the individual that is in the possession or under the
control of the organisation; and
(b)
information about the ways in which the personal data mentioned in
paragraph (a) has been or may have been used or disclosed by the
organisation within a year before the date of the request.
(2) An organisation is not required to provide an individual with the individual’s
personal data or other information under subsection (1) in respect of the matters specified
in the Fifth Schedule.
(3) Subject to subsection (3A), an organisation must not provide an individual with
the individual’s personal data or other information under subsection (1) if the provision
of that personal data or other information (as the case may be) could reasonably be
expected to —
(a)
threaten the safety or physical or mental health of an individual other than
