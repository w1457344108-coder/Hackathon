386AIA.—(1) A company or foreign company must, at the prescribed frequency,
give a notice to each registrable controller whose particulars are stated in the company’s
or foreign company’s register of controllers for the following purposes:
(a)
to require the registrable controller to confirm whether or not a relevant
change has occurred and, if the change has occurred, to —
(i)
state the date of the change; and
(ii)
provide the particulars of the change;
(b)
to require the registrable controller to confirm whether the stated
particulars of the registrable controller are correct and, if not, to provide
