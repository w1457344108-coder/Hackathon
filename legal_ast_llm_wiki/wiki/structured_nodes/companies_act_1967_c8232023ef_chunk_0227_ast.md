(b)
every director of the company has paid to the company on each of the
shares taken or contracted to be taken by him or her, and for which he or
she is liable to pay in cash, a proportion equal to the proportion payable on
application and allotment on the shares payable in cash; and
(c)
there has been lodged with the Registrar a declaration in the prescribed
form by —
(i)
the secretary or one of the directors of the company; or
(ii)
a registered qualified individual authorised by the company,
verifying that paragraph (b) has been complied with.
