## Original Excerpt

```text
d 396A —
“company” includes a corporation which is required to keep company records
under this Act;
“company record” means any register, index, minute book, accounting record,
minute or other document required by this Act to be kept by a company;
“in electronic form” means in the form of an electronic record as defined in
section 2(1) of the Electronic Transactions Act 2010;
“in hard copy form” means in a paper form or similar form capable of being read.
[36/2014]
Duty to take precautions against falsification
396.—(1) Where company records are kept otherwise than in hard copy form,
reasonable precautions must be taken for —
(a)
ensuring the proper maintenance and authenticity of the company records;
```
