(4) This section is in addition to and not in derogation of any other written law or rule
of law relating to the duty or liability of directors or officers of a company.
(5) In this section —
“officer” includes a person who at any time has been an officer of the company;
“agent” includes a banker, solicitor or auditor of the company and any person who
at any time has been a banker, solicitor or auditor of the company.
Powers of directors
157A.—(1) The business of a company is to be managed by, or under the direction or
supervision of, the directors.
[36/2014]
(2) The directors may exercise all the powers of a company except any power that
this Act or the constitution of the company requires the company to exercise in general
meeting.
