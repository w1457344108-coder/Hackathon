company), the Court may either by the order approving the compromise or arrangement

(d)
the dissolution, without winding up, of the transferor company;
(e)
the provision to be made for any persons who, within such time and in such
manner as the Court directs, dissent from the compromise or arrangement;
(f)
such incidental, consequential and supplemental matters as are necessary to
secure that the reconstruction or amalgamation is fully and effectively
carried out.
