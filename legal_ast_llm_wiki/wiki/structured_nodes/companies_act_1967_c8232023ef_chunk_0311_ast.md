Authority for selective off-market acquisition
76D.—(1) A company may make a purchase or acquisition of its own shares
otherwise than on a securities exchange and not in accordance with an equal access
scheme (called in this section a selective off-market purchase) if the purchase or
acquisition is made in accordance with an agreement authorised in advance under
subsection (2).
[36/2014]
(2) The terms of the agreement for a selective off-market purchase must be authorised
by a special resolution of the company, with no votes being cast by any person whose
