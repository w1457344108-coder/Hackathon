the exercise of some power exercisable by it without the consent or concurrence of any
other person can appoint or remove all or a majority of the directors, and for the purposes
of this provision that other corporation is deemed to have power to make such an
appointment if —
(a)
a person cannot be appointed as a director without the exercise in his or her
favour by that other corporation of such a power; or
(b)
a person’s appointment as a director follows necessarily from his or her
being a director or other officer of that other corporation.
