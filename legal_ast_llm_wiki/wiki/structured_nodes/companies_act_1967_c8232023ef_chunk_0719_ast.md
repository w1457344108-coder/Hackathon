Resignation of non-public interest company auditors
205AA.—(1) An auditor of a non-public interest company (other than a company
which is a subsidiary company of a public interest company) may resign before the end
of the term of office for which the auditor was appointed by giving the company a notice
of resignation in writing.
[36/2014]
(2) Where a notice of resignation is given under subsection (1), the auditor’s term of
office expires —
(a)
at the end of the day on which notice is given to the company; or
(b)
if the notice specifies a time on a later day for the purpose, at that time.
[36/2014]
(3) Within 14 days beginning on the date on which a company receives a notice of
resignation under subsection (1), the company must lodge with the Registrar a
notification of that fact in such form as the Registrar may require.
[36/2014]
(4) In this section and sections 205AB, 205AC and 205AF —
“non-public interest company” means a company other than a public interest
company;
