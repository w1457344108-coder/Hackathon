Obligations of data intermediary of public agency
26E. Where an organisation —
(a)
is a data intermediary processing personal data on behalf of and for
the purposes of a public agency; and
(b)
has reason to believe that a data breach has occurred in relation to
that personal data,
the organisation must, without undue delay, notify the public agency of the
occurrence of the data breach.”.
