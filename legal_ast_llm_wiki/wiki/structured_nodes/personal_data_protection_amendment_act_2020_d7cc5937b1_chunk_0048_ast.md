22. The principal Act is amended by inserting, immediately after section 48, the

“PART IXA
DICTIONARY ATTACKS AND
ADDRESS-HARVESTING SOFTWARE
Interpretation of this Part
48A.—(1) In this Part, unless the context otherwise requires —
“address-harvesting software” means software that is specifically designed
or marketed for use for —
(a)
searching the Internet for telephone numbers; and
(b)
collecting, compiling, capturing or otherwise harvesting those
telephone numbers;
“applicable message” means a message with a Singapore link that is sent to
any applicable telephone number;
“applicable telephone number” means a telephone number that is generated
or obtained through the use of —
(a)
a dictionary attack; or
(b)
address-harvesting software;
