Duty of company to keep consents of directors and secretaries
173C. Every company must keep at its registered office —
(a)
in respect of each director —
(i)
a signed copy of his or her consent to act as director;
(ii)
a statement that he or she is not disqualified to act as director
under this Act or under any other written law; and
(iii)
documentary evidence (if any) of any change in his or her name;
and
(b)
in respect of a secretary, a signed copy of his or her consent to act as
secretary.
[36/2014]
Saving and transitional provisions for existing particulars of directors, chief
