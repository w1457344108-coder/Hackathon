(2) In proceedings for an offence under subsection (1), it is a defence to the

(a)
that —
(i)
the personal data in the possession or under the control of
the organisation or public agency (as the case may be) that
was disclosed was, at the time of the disclosure, publicly
available; and
(ii)
where the personal data was publicly available solely
because of an applicable contravention, the accused did not
know, and was not reckless as to whether, that was the
case;
(b)
the accused disclosed, or caused the disclosure of, personal data in
the possession or under the control of the organisation or public
agency, as the case may be —
(i)
as permitted or required by or under an Act or other law
(apart from this Act);
(ii)
as authorised or required by an order of court;
(iii)
in the reasonable belief that, and was not reckless as to
whether, the accused had the legal right to do so; or
(iv)
in any other circumstances, or for any other purpose,
prescribed.
